# Accepted-Stock Dry-Solids Measurement and Traceability for Recycled Containerboard Mills

This project develops a low-cost, phased framework for quantifying and tracing accepted dry stock in recycled-containerboard production.

The framework addresses a common material-accounting gap: mills may know the wet mass of incoming recovered paper and the weight of finished jumbo reels, but may not continuously quantify the dry mass of cleaned accepted stock leaving stock preparation.

The system calculates accepted dry-stock mass flow from slurry flow, density, and stock consistency:

m_dry = Q × ρ × C

The intended outputs include:
- Accepted dry stock (kg/h, t/shift, t/day)
- Reject mass and reject rate
- Estimated clean-stock yield
- Pulp-to-jumbo conversion ratio
- Material-accounting variance
- Supplier/waste-category performance indicators
- Cost per usable tonne of fiber

## Project scope
- Recycled containerboard: testliner, white-top liner, and corrugating medium/fluting
- Recovered paper/OCC and mixed-waste furnish
- Low-cost proof of concept through to industrial-scale implementation
- Case-study application: Mega Paper and Boards Limited, Kenya

## Implementation pathway
1. Manual baseline and process mapping
2. Research/proof-of-concept data collection
3. Minimum viable industrial instrumentation
4. Full traceability and yield reconciliation
